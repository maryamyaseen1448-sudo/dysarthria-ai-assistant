About this research prototype

This prototype demonstrates speech input, dysarthric speech recognition using an open-source Whisper model, user-specific text corrections, confidence-based clarification, and clear text/voice output. Dataset recordings are used for technical testing.

Research note: confidence is an estimated model-derived indicator, not a clinically validated probability. Results depend on dataset quality, speaker variation, language, and recording conditions. This prototype does not diagnose or treat dysarthria.
